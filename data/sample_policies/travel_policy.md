# Corporate Travel and Expense Policy
**Effective Date:** March 15, 2024
**Applies to:** All employees undertaking approved business travel

## 1. Travel Booking
*   All flights and accommodations must be booked through the company's designated travel portal (TravelDesk).
*   **Domestic Flights:** Economy class only.
*   **International Flights:** Economy class for flights under 8 hours. Premium Economy or Business class may be approved by a VP for flights exceeding 8 hours.

## 2. Accommodation
*   Standard room rates should not exceed:
    *   **Tier 1 Cities (New York, London, Tokyo):** $250 per night
    *   **Tier 2 Cities:** $150 per night

## 3. Daily Per Diem / Meals
*   Employees are provided a daily allowance for meals and incidentals during travel.
    *   Domestic Travel: $60/day
    *   International Travel: $100/day
*   Alcohol consumption is not reimbursable under standard meal expenses unless part of an approved client entertainment event.

## 4. Expense Reporting
*   All expenses must be submitted via the Concur system within **15 days** of returning from travel.
*   Receipts are mandatory for any single expense exceeding $25.
