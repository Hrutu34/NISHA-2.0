# Shift and On-Call Allowance Policy
**Effective Date:** February 1, 2024
**Applies to:** Support, IT Ops, and designated shift workers

## 1. Shift Timings
*   **Morning Shift:** 06:00 AM to 02:00 PM
*   **General Shift:** 09:00 AM to 05:00 PM
*   **Evening Shift:** 02:00 PM to 10:00 PM
*   **Night Shift:** 10:00 PM to 06:00 AM

## 2. Shift Allowances
*   **Evening Shift Allowance:** $15 per shift.
*   **Night Shift Allowance:** $30 per shift.
*   General and Morning shifts do not attract any additional shift allowance.

## 3. Weekend Working
*   Employees rostered to work on a Saturday or Sunday as part of their regular shift schedule will receive a **weekend premium of $50 per day**, in addition to any applicable shift allowance.
*   If a weekend shift constitutes overtime (beyond 40 hours a week), standard overtime rates (1.5x hourly rate) will apply instead of the weekend premium.

## 4. On-Call Allowance
*   Employees placed on standby/on-call during weekends or holidays receive a flat rate of $100 per day, regardless of whether they are called into work.
