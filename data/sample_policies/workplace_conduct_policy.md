# Workplace Conduct and Grievance Redressal Policy
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2024
**Applies to:** All employees, contractors, and vendors

## 1. Professional Conduct & Dress Code
*   **Dress Code:** Smart casuals from Monday to Thursday; casual attire is permitted on Fridays.
*   **ID Badge:** Employees must wear their RFID company badges visibly at all times on company premises. Lost badges must be reported to building security immediately ($15 replacement fee).

## 2. Anti-Harassment & POSH Policy
*   The company enforces a zero-tolerance policy against sexual harassment, discrimination, or workplace bullying.
*   Complaints can be submitted confidentially to the Internal Complaints Committee (ICC) at `posh-committee@company.com`.
*   All inquiries are conducted with strict confidentiality and concluded within **30 days**.

## 3. Grievance Escalation Process
*   **Level 1 (Direct Resolution):** Discuss the concern directly with your immediate Reporting Manager.
*   **Level 2 (HR Business Partner):** If unresolved within 5 working days, escalate to your assigned HRBP.
*   **Level 3 (Ethics Hotline):** For whistleblowing or severe integrity violations, file an anonymous ticket via the Internal Ethics Portal (`ethics.company.internal`).
