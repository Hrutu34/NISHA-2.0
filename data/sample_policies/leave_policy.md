# Comprehensive Leave and Attendance Policy
**Policy Reference:** HR-POL-2024-001  
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2024  
**Applies to:** All regular and probationary employees  

## 1. Annual Leave (Privilege Leave / Earned Leave)
*   **Accrual Rate:** Employees accrue **1.66 days** of paid Annual Leave per completed month of active service, totaling **20 working days per calendar year**.
*   **Carryover Rules:** A maximum of **10 unused Annual Leave days** can be carried forward into the subsequent calendar year. Any unused balance exceeding 10 days on December 31 will automatically lapse without financial encashment.
*   **Advance Notice:** Leave requests exceeding 3 consecutive days must be submitted via the HRMS portal at least **14 calendar days in advance** and require explicit approval from the reporting manager.

## 2. Sick & Medical Leave
*   **Allotment:** Every employee receives **10 paid Sick Leave days** at the start of each calendar year (prorated for new joiners based on start quarter).
*   **Documentation Threshold:** A valid medical certificate signed by a licensed practitioner is mandatory for any sick leave absence extending **beyond 3 consecutive working days**.
*   **Non-Accrual:** Sick leave cannot be carried forward, encashed, or transferred upon separation.

## 3. Parental & Family Care Leave
### Maternity Leave
*   Female employees with at least 80 days of active service prior to the expected delivery date are entitled to **26 weeks of fully paid maternity leave**.
*   Up to 8 weeks may be availed prior to the expected date of delivery, with the remaining balance utilized post-delivery.
*   In the unfortunate event of a miscarriage or medical termination of pregnancy, up to **6 weeks of paid leave** is granted immediately upon submission of medical proof.

### Paternity Leave
*   Eligible male employees are entitled to **15 working days of paid paternity leave**.
*   This leave can be taken in a single block or split into a maximum of two blocks, but must be fully utilized within **6 months of the child's birth or legal adoption date**.

### Adoption & Surrogacy Leave
*   Primary adoptive parents of a child under 1 year of age are granted **12 weeks of paid leave**.

## 4. Bereavement & Compassionate Leave
*   Employees are granted up to **5 paid days** in the event of the loss of an immediate family member (spouse, child, parent, sibling, or parent-in-law).
*   Up to **2 additional travel days** may be approved by HR if interstate or international travel is required.

## 5. Unscheduled Absences & Loss of Pay (LOP)
*   Absence without prior notification or approval for **3 consecutive working days** will be marked as *Unauthorized Absence*.
*   Failing to report for 7 consecutive days without communication will be treated as voluntary abandonment of employment under Section 8 of the Employee Agreement.
