# Employee Leave Policy
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2024
**Applies to:** All full-time employees

## 1. Annual Leave (Privilege Leave)
*   Employees are entitled to **20 days** of paid Annual Leave per calendar year.
*   Leave accrues at the rate of 1.66 days per month.
*   A maximum of 10 days can be carried forward to the next calendar year. Unused leave beyond this will lapse.
*   Requests for more than 3 consecutive days must be submitted at least 2 weeks in advance.

## 2. Sick Leave
*   Employees receive **10 days** of sick leave annually.
*   A medical certificate from a registered practitioner is required for sick leave extending beyond 3 consecutive days.
*   Sick leave cannot be carried forward or encashed.

## 3. Parental Leave
### Maternity Leave
*   Eligible female employees are entitled to **26 weeks** of paid maternity leave.
*   Can be taken up to 8 weeks before the expected delivery date.

### Paternity Leave
*   Eligible male employees are entitled to **15 days** of paid paternity leave.
*   Must be consumed within 6 months of the child's birth.

## 4. Bereavement Leave
*   Employees are granted up to **5 days** of paid leave in the event of the death of an immediate family member (spouse, child, parent, or sibling).
