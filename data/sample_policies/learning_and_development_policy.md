# Learning, Certification, and Development Policy
**Effective Date:** March 1, 2024
**Applies to:** All employees post-onboarding

## 1. Annual Learning Budget
*   Every full-time employee is allocated an annual personal development budget of **$1,000 per calendar year**.
*   This budget can be used for professional certifications (e.g., AWS, GCP, PMP), industry conferences, and approved online courses.

## 2. Eligibility & Approvals
*   New joiners can apply for course reimbursements after completing **30 days** of tenure.
*   Manager pre-approval is required before purchasing any course or registering for an examination.

## 3. Reimbursement Process
*   Reimbursements are processed upon submitting a proof of passing/completion certificate along with the official payment receipt.
*   Unused learning budgets do not carry forward to subsequent years.

## 4. Retention Clause
*   If an employee receives certification sponsorship exceeding **$500** and voluntarily leaves the company within **6 months** of certification, the reimbursement amount will be recovered in the final settlement.
