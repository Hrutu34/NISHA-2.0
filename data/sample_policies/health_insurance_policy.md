# Group Health Insurance, OPD, and Wellness Benefits
**Policy Reference:** HR-POL-2024-004  
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2024  
**Applies to:** Full-time employees and registered dependents  

## 1. Group Medical Coverage (GMC)
The company provides comprehensive family-floater hospitalization coverage underwritten by our designated insurance partner.

*   **Default Base Cover:** **$25,000 (or INR 10,00,000 equivalent)** per family per policy year, 100% company-subsidized.
*   **Voluntary Top-Up:** Employees may purchase additional voluntary coverage of up to $50,000 at corporate discounted premium rates deducted from monthly payroll.

## 2. Eligible Dependents & Enrollment Deadlines
*   **Defined Family:** Self + Spouse/Domestic Partner + up to 2 dependent children (unmarried, aged up to 25) + up to 2 dependent parents OR parents-in-law (cross-combination is not permitted).
*   **Mandatory Enrollment Deadline:** New hires must complete dependent registration on the Insurance Portal within **30 calendar days of joining**.
*   **Late Registration Penalty:** Failure to register within 30 days locks dependent records until the annual company-wide Open Enrollment window in October.

## 3. Hospitalization Procedures & Submissions
*   **Cashless Claims:** Available at all listed Network Hospitals. The employee must present their digital TPA e-card and photo ID at the hospital admission desk at least 48 hours before planned procedures or within 24 hours for emergency admissions.
*   **Reimbursement Claims:** For non-network facilities, claims must be formally submitted to HR/TPA with original discharge summaries, payment receipts, and pharmacy bills within **30 days of hospital discharge**.

## 4. Annual OPD, Dental & Vision Allowance
*   Every employee is entitled to an annual **Outpatient Department (OPD) Wellness Allowance of $350 per financial year**.
*   Eligible expenses include: Routine dental cleanings, prescription eyeglasses/contact lenses, preventive health checkups, and diagnostic blood tests.
*   Claims must be filed under the "Health & Wellness" category on the expense management portal with itemized invoices.

## 5. Employee Assistance Program (EAP)
*   Confidential, 24/7 mental health counseling and life coaching is provided through our wellness partner, *CareBridge*.
*   Up to **6 free individual sessions per year** are available to each employee and their immediate family members.
