# Probation and Confirmation Policy
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2024
**Applies to:** All newly hired full-time employees

## 1. Probation Period
*   All new joiners undergo a standard probationary period of **90 days (3 months)** starting from their official date of joining.
*   The probation period allows both the employee and the manager to assess role fit, performance, and cultural alignment.

## 2. Review and Confirmation Process
*   **30-Day Check-in:** An informal review between the manager and employee to discuss initial onboarding progress.
*   **60-Day Mid-Review:** A formal checkpoint to highlight strengths and identify performance gaps.
*   **90-Day Final Evaluation:** A formal performance appraisal. If successful, HR issues an official Letter of Confirmation within 7 business days.

## 3. Extension of Probation
*   If performance metrics or attendance criteria are not met, the reporting manager may extend the probation period by an additional **30 to 60 days**.
*   A maximum of one extension is permitted.

## 4. Notice Period During Probation
*   Either the employee or the company may terminate employment by providing a written notice of **15 days** (or salary in lieu thereof), compared to the standard 60-day notice period for confirmed employees.
