# Global Employee Transfer and Relocation Policy
**Policy Reference:** HR-POL-2024-009  
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2024  
**Applies to:** Full-time permanent employees worldwide  

## 1. Transfer Types & Objectives
The company recognizes two primary categories of employee transfers:
*   **Company-Initiated Transfer:** Driven by project staffing requirements, entity restructuring, or new office expansions.
*   **Self-Initiated Transfer (Internal Mobility):** Requested voluntarily by an employee for personal reasons or career progression via the Internal Job Posting (IJP) portal.

## 2. Eligibility Criteria for Self-Initiated Transfers
To apply for a lateral or location transfer, an employee must satisfy all of the following requirements:
1.  **Tenure:** Completed a minimum of **18 continuous months** in their current department and base office location.
2.  **Performance Rating:** Achieved a minimum rating of "Meets Expectations" (Level 3 or higher) in the most recent annual performance review cycle.
3.  **Disciplinary Standing:** Must not be under any active Performance Improvement Plan (PIP) or active disciplinary proceedings within the last 6 months.
4.  **Notice to Manager:** Must inform their current Line Manager prior to formally submitting an application on the IJP portal.

## 3. Relocation Benefits & Compensation Matrix
Relocation benefits apply only when the transfer distance exceeds **100 miles (160 km)** from the current base office location.

| Benefit Component | Single Employee Tier | Employee with Dependents (Spouse/Children) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Lump Sum Relocation Grant** | $3,000 (taxable) | $5,000 (taxable) |
| **Temporary Corporate Housing** | Up to 14 consecutive calendar days | Up to 21 consecutive calendar days |
| **Household Goods Shipment** | Up to 3,500 lbs via partner logistics | Up to 6,000 lbs via partner logistics |
| **Vehicle Transport** | 1 personal vehicle | Up to 2 personal vehicles |
| **House Hunting Leave** | 2 paid business days | 3 paid business days |

## 4. International Relocation Provisions
For cross-border international transfers:
*   The company will cover 100% of visa, legal filing, and work permit application fees for the employee and legal dependents.
*   A one-time settling-in allowance of $2,500 will be credited upon arrival to cover initial utility deposits and local registrations.
*   Tax equalization advisory sessions will be provided by our third-party tax consultants.

## 5. Clawback & Repayment Obligation
All relocation grants and moving expenses paid by the company are subject to a **12-month retention agreement**:
*   If the employee voluntarily resigns or is terminated for cause within **6 months** of the transfer date, **100% of the total relocation cost** must be repaid.
*   If the departure occurs between **6 and 12 months**, the repayment is prorated at **50%**.
*   Clawback deductions will be directly processed from the employee's final salary settlement.

## 6. Escalations & Exception Approval
Any exceptions to eligibility requirements (e.g., tenure waivers under exceptional compassionate grounds) must be approved in writing by both the **Department Head (VP level)** and the **Chief Human Resources Officer (CHRO)**.
