# Employee Transfer and Relocation Policy
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2024
**Applies to:** Employees undergoing company-initiated or approved self-initiated transfers

## 1. Eligibility
*   Employees must have completed at least **18 months** in their current role and location to be eligible for a self-initiated transfer.
*   Performance rating must be "Meets Expectations" or higher in the most recent appraisal cycle.

## 2. Relocation Allowance
For company-initiated transfers requiring relocation of more than 100 miles:
*   **Single Employees:** One-time allowance of $3,000.
*   **Employees with Dependents:** One-time allowance of $5,000.
*   The company will cover the direct cost of moving household goods via a partnered logistics vendor up to 5,000 lbs.

## 3. Temporary Accommodation
*   The company will provide up to **14 days** of temporary corporate housing or hotel accommodation at the new location.

## 4. Clawback Clause
*   If an employee resigns within **12 months** of receiving relocation benefits, they must repay 100% of the relocation allowance and moving costs.
