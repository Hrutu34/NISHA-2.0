# Hybrid and Remote Work Policy
**Effective Date:** February 15, 2024
**Applies to:** Full-time corporate employees

## 1. Hybrid Model Guidelines
*   Employees operate on a **3+2 Hybrid Model**: A minimum of **3 days per week in-office** and up to **2 days work-from-home (WFH)**.
*   **Core Team Days:** Tuesday and Thursday are mandatory in-office collaboration days across all engineering and product teams.

## 2. Working Hours and Core Availability
*   Standard working hours are 8 hours per day between 08:00 AM and 07:00 PM local time.
*   **Core Collaboration Window:** All remote employees must be accessible via Slack/Teams and available for meetings between **10:00 AM and 04:00 PM**.

## 3. Work-From-Home Setup Stipend
*   New joiners are eligible for a one-time WFH ergonomics allowance of up to **$300** to purchase an office chair, desk, or peripheral accessories.
*   Claims must be submitted with valid tax invoices via the expense portal within **45 days of joining**.

## 4. Internet Reimbursement
*   Employees working in a hybrid or remote role can claim a monthly internet reimbursement of up to **$40 per month**.
